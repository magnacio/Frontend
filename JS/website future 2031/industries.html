PAGE 03 / BEYOND IT

Chennai’s future is multi-industry.

The wider Chennai region is not only a technology centre. Its industrial base includes automobiles and EVs, electronics, healthcare and life sciences, aerospace and defence, logistics and advanced manufacturing. The strongest 2031 scenario connects these sectors with software rather than treating them as competitors.

2031 CAREER IDEA

Do not prepare only for a job title. Build a technical base and understand an industry.



A developer who understands vehicles, a data engineer who understands healthcare, or a cybersecurity engineer who understands factories can bring more value than someone who only knows a narrow tool.
