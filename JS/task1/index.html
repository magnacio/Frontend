By 2031, technology becomes less visible — and more useful.

Intelligent Work

02

Smarter Cities

Connected sensors help manage energy, traffic, water, security and infrastructure with better efficiency.

03

Personal Learning

02 / THE PROBLEM

Progress creates new challenges.

01

Privacy & Security

More connected services mean more sensitive information that must be protected.

02

Changing Jobs

Automation changes repetitive roles and increases the importance of continuous learning.

03

Digital Overload

More apps and devices can create complexity instead of convenience.

Human-first technology.

Future systems should combine intelligent automation with human control. Privacy and cybersecurity must be designed in from the beginning, while education helps people continuously develop new skills.
