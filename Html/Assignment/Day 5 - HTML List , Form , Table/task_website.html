Bangalore

The city boasts a dynamic ecosystem that continuously attracts global tech conglomerates and fosters groundbreaking domestic innovations.

Hyderabad: Cyberabad's Innovation Hub

Hyderabad is one of India's premier IT and tech powerhouses, contributing over 15% of the country's total software exports. Anchored by the tech corridors of HITEC City, Gachibowli, and the Financial District, the city hosts over 700 IT companies and boasts the largest development centers for both Microsoft and Amazon outside the US
