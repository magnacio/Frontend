Five Paragraphs with Formatting Tags

Bold text is often used to highlight important keywords within a paragraph, drawing the reader's eye to key information.

Italic text is commonly used to emphasize a word or phrase, or to denote titles of works such as books and films.

You can combine bold and italic together when a phrase needs extra emphasis in a paragraph.

The highlight tag is useful for marking text that is relevant to the current context, such as search results.

Chemical formulas like H2O and mathematical expressions like x2 use subscript and superscript tags respectively.
