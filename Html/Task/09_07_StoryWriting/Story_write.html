The Shawshank Redemption

In 1947, banker Andy Dufresne is wrongfully convicted of murdering his wife and her lover, receiving two consecutive life sentences. He is sent to Shawshank State Prison, a brutal facility run by the cruel Warden Norton and the sadistic Captain Byron Hadley. Initially quiet and isolated, Andy forms a deep, lifelong friendship with Red, a contraband smuggler and fellow inmate. After using his financial expertise to help Hadley with a tax problem, Andy becomes an invaluable asset to the guards and the warden. He is reassigned to the prison library, which he dramatically expands, while laundering money for Norton’s corrupt business scams. Over two decades, Andy maintains his dignity and brings hope to his fellow inmates, even after Norton destroys evidence that could prove his innocence. One morning in 1966, the guards discover Andy's cell is empty; he has meticulously tunneled through the wall using a small rock hammer over 19 years. He escapes through a sewage pipe, exposes the warden's corruption to the press, and steals the laundered funds. Warden Norton commits suicide to avoid arrest, while Hadley is taken into custody. After finally receiving parole, Red follows a series of clues Andy left behind and travels to a beach in Mexico, where the two friends happily reunite as free men.
